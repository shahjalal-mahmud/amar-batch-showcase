## 💝 Why Your Contribution Matters

Every teacher who uses Amar Batch helps us understand what works and what needs improvement. Your feedback shapes the app and helps us build something truly useful for Bangladeshi teachers.

**By contributing, you're helping:**

- Save other teachers time and effort
- Reduce stress around fees and attendance
- Improve communication between teachers and parents
- Build a tool that respects teachers' time and money
