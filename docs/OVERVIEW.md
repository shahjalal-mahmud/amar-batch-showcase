# Overview — Amar Batch

## 📱 Product Concept

**Amar Batch** is an offline-first Android app designed for private teachers and small batch tutors in Bangladesh.

The product helps teachers manage:

- Student records
- Daily attendance
- Monthly fees
- Exam results
- Parent communication
- Basic business operations

without needing complicated software, constant internet, or technical expertise.

The goal is simple:

> Help teachers stay organized using a lightweight mobile app that feels faster and easier than maintaining physical notebooks.

---

## 🎯 Problem Being Solved

Many private teachers in Bangladesh still manage their batches using:

- Paper diaries
- Attendance notebooks
- Manual fee tracking
- Memory-based record keeping
- Unorganized SMS communication

This creates common problems:

| Challenge                   | Result                         |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Forgetting attendance       | Parents lose trust             |
| Manual fee tracking         | Payment confusion              |
| Lost notebooks              | Important records disappear    |
| No exam history             | Hard to track student progress |
| No structured communication | Parents stay uninformed        |

Amar Batch is designed to reduce these operational problems with a simple mobile workflow.

---

## 👨‍🏫 Intended Users

Amar Batch is primarily designed for:

- Home tuition teachers
- Small coaching tutors
- Freelance subject teachers
- Single-teacher batch operators
- Teachers handling 10–200 students

### Current Focus

The current product direction focuses on:

- Individual teachers
- Offline usability
- Low-cost operation
- Simple daily workflows
- Mobile-first experience

### Not Intended For (Current Version)

- Large coaching centers
- Multi-teacher institutions
- Schools or colleges
- Enterprise education systems

---

## 🌟 Product Philosophy

Amar Batch is intentionally designed around simplicity.

Instead of building a large, enterprise-style management system, the product focuses on:

- Fast daily usage
- Low learning curve
- Minimal setup
- Offline reliability
- Affordable pricing
- Real teacher workflows

### Core Principles

| Principle           | Description                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Offline First       | Core features work without internet              |
| Simple Over Complex | Focus on essential workflows                     |
| Teacher Controlled  | Teachers manage their own data and communication |
| Low Friction        | Daily operations should take seconds             |
| Privacy Focused     | Data stays primarily on the teacher's device     |

---

## 🧩 Core Feature Areas

### Student Management

Teachers can organize students by class and shift while maintaining structured records.

### Attendance Tracking

Quick attendance workflow optimized for batch teaching environments.

### Fee Management

Monthly fee collection and due tracking for tuition operations.

### Exam & Performance Tracking

Record exam marks and monitor student progress history.

### Parent Communication

SMS-based notifications using the teacher's own mobile SIM.

### Backup & Restore

Optional cloud backup integration for safer long-term storage.

---

## 📲 Product Experience

The app is designed around a very short daily workflow:

1. Open app
2. Select batch
3. Mark attendance
4. Save

Most common actions are designed to be completed within seconds.

The product intentionally avoids:

- Complex dashboards
- Heavy analytics
- Enterprise workflows
- Feature overload
- Internet dependency

---

## 💰 Business Model

Amar Batch follows a paid software model with a free trial period.

### Monetization Approach

- Free trial for new users
- Affordable yearly license
- Manual payment activation
- No advertisements
- No data selling
- No feature locking during trial

The product is designed as a sustainable commercial software business targeted at the Bangladeshi education support market.
