## 3. How We Store Your Information

### 3.1 Local Storage (Primary)

**All your data is stored locally on your device** using SQLite database (Room):

- **Location**: Internal app storage (not accessible to other apps)
- **Encryption**: Android's built-in app sandboxing
- **Duration**: Until you uninstall the app or delete data
- **Access**: Only you and the app can access this data

**Data Stored Locally:**

- All student information
- Attendance records
- Fee transactions
- Exam results
- Expenses
- SMS queue
- App settings

### 3.2 Cloud Storage (Optional)

If you enable **Google Drive backup**:

- **Platform**: Your personal Google Drive account
- **Location**: Google's servers (subject to Google's privacy policy)
- **Encryption**: Files encrypted before upload
- **Access**: Only you can access (your Google account)
- **Control**: You can delete backups anytime from Google Drive
- **Frequency**: Automatic (when connected to internet) or manual

**Important**: We do not have access to your Google Drive. The backup file is stored in your own Google Drive account, and only you can access it.

### 3.3 Developer Server (Minimal)

We store minimal information on our server for license management only:

**Data Stored:**

- Teacher name
- Phone number
- Device ID
- License status (trial/paid/blocked)
- Trial start date
- Expiry date
- Last sync timestamp

**Purpose**: Verify subscriptions and manage trial periods  
**Security**: Encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest  
**Retention**: For the duration of your subscription + 1 year

## 5. SMS and Parent Communication

### 5.1 How SMS Works

- **Sent From**: Your phone's SIM card
- **Cost**: Charged by your mobile carrier (not us)
- **Control**: You enable/disable SMS anytime
- **Content**: Generated from your custom templates
