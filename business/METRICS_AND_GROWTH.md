# Amar Batch — Metrics & Growth Strategy

## 📊 Overview

This document defines how Amar Batch measures success, tracks product growth, evaluates business health, and improves long-term sustainability.

The focus is intentionally simple:

- Real usage over vanity numbers
- Retention over downloads
- Teacher satisfaction over aggressive monetization
- Sustainable growth over rapid scaling

Amar Batch is built for a specific underserved audience: Bangladeshi private teachers. Growth strategy and metrics are designed around that reality.

---

# 1. PRODUCT GROWTH PHILOSOPHY

## Core Principle

> "If teachers continue using Amar Batch daily, growth will happen naturally."

The product depends heavily on:

- Habit formation
- Daily utility
- Word-of-mouth referrals
- Trust
- Reliability

The goal is NOT explosive startup-style growth.

The goal IS:

- Sustainable adoption
- High retention
- Long-term trust
- Real usefulness

---

# 2. NORTH STAR METRIC

## 🎯 Primary Success Metric

### Active Teachers Taking Attendance Weekly

This is the single most important product metric.

### Why This Matters

Attendance is:

- The most frequent teacher activity
- The habit-forming core feature
- The gateway to all other features

If teachers regularly take attendance:

- They are actively using the app
- Student records stay updated
- Fees become valuable
- SMS becomes useful
- Retention increases naturally

---

## Target Benchmarks

| Stage        | Weekly Active Attendance Users |
| ------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Beta Testing | 5–20                           |
| Early Launch | 50–100                         |
| Year 1       | 300+                           |
| Year 2       | 1,000+                         |

## 💵 Unit Economics

### Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

Expected revenue from one teacher over multiple years.

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Cost to acquire one paying teacher.

### LTV:CAC Ratio

Target:

- Greater than 5:1

Because:

- Marketing cost is expected to stay low
- Word-of-mouth is primary growth channel

Amar Batch grows through usefulness, not aggressive marketing.

The strategy is simple:

1. Solve real teacher problems
2. Make daily tasks faster
3. Build trust through reliability
4. Keep pricing affordable
5. Let satisfied teachers spread the product naturally
