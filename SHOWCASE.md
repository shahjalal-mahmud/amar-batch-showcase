## 🎯 Skills Demonstrated

### Android Development

- **Jetpack Compose** — Modern declarative UI with Material 3
- **Clean Architecture** — Separation of concerns (UI-ViewModel-Repository-Data)
- **MVVM Pattern** — State management with StateFlow
- **Room Database** — Complex relational schema with migrations
- **Dependency Injection** — Koin for lightweight DI
- **Coroutines & Flow** — Async operations and reactive streams
- **WorkManager** — Reliable background jobs with retry logic

### Architecture Decisions

- **Offline-first design** — Works 100% without internet
- **Repository pattern** — Single source of truth
- **Unidirectional data flow** — Predictable state management

### Database Design

- **17 normalized tables** — With proper relationships
- **Soft delete pattern** — Data preservation
- **Complex queries** — With Room Relations and Transactions
- **Indexing strategy** — Performance optimization

### Product & Business

- **Commercial product** — Teachers pay yearly subscription
- **Manual licensing system** — Device-bound, trial period
- **Target market identification** — Bangladeshi batch teachers
