## 🚨 Escalation Process

If your issue is not resolved within the promised response time:

1. **First escalation:** Reply to the same email/WhatsApp thread (do not create new ticket)
2. **Second escalation:** Send a follow-up with "URGENT - [Original Subject]"
3. **Final escalation:** Call during emergency hours

**We take support seriously.** If we fail to respond within the promised time, please call or email again. Technical issues, limited team size, or high volume may occasionally cause delays.
